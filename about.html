Our History

Charity No. 208709 · Est. 1962

The beginning

We make a living
by what we get,
but we make a life
by what we give.

— Winston Churchill

In 1962, a small group of tea traders gathered in the City of London with a simple conviction: people who have given their working lives to the UK tea trade should never face hardship alone.

They formed the UK Tea Trade Benevolent Society — a registered charity that offers welfare, companionship and practical support to anyone who has worked in the trade, whether they are still behind the desk or long since retired.

More than sixty years later, the mission has not changed. We are still here, still listening, still helping — quietly.

How we help
01

Well-being check-ins

A friendly voice on the phone, a visit when it matters. We keep in regular contact with members so that nobody is left isolated or forgotten.

02

Practical advocacy

Navigating benefits, pensions, council support and grant applications can be bewildering. We walk alongside members through the process — hands-on, not hands-off.

03

Direct financial relief

When unexpected hardship strikes — an emergency boiler, a funeral, a gap between payments — the Society can step in with confidential, no-strings financial support.

From the trading floor
to the kitchen table.

Whether you blended, brokered, packed, shipped or simply made the tea — if the UK tea trade was your livelihood, this Society is for you.
