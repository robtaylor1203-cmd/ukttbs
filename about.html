Chapter 01 · Our purpose

A lifetime of dedication deserves a lifetime of care.

The UK Tea Trade Benevolent Society is a charitable organisation dedicated to supporting everyone who has worked within the UK tea trade. We understand that a lifetime of dedication to this unique industry can sometimes lead to unexpected challenges later in life.

Whether you are currently active in the trade or have long since retired, our commitment is simple: ensure that you are not left to face hardships alone. At our core, we offer more than just financial aid — we offer community, compassion, and practical support.

We conduct regular well-being checks to ensure our members are healthy, secure, and connected with others who share a passion for the tea trade. We provide hands-on assistance through the sometimes-complex process of applying for government grants, pensions, and other financial support — helping you navigate these systems with clarity and confidence.

When life brings unexpected difficulties, the Society steps in with direct financial support to help members manage hard times. We believe in the power of a caring community to uplift its members.
