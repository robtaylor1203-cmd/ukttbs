Chapter 01 · Mission

To ensure no one who has served is
left to face hardship alone.

We are the safety net and the friendly voice for people whose working lives have been given to tea.

Chapter 02 · Vision

From Trainee, to Retiree —
we care for those who have cared for our industry.

A network that protects, supports and celebrates the people who have built the trade's rich heritage.
