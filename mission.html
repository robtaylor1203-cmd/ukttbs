Mission · Vision · Values

Quiet principles, steadily kept.

The short version of who we are — and what we stand for.

Chapter 01 · Mission

To ensure no one who has served the UK tea trade is left to face hardship alone.

We are the safety net and the friendly voice for people whose working lives have been given to tea.

Chapter 02 · Vision

A connected, caring tea community — from first shift to final years.

A network that protects, supports and celebrates the people who have built the trade's rich heritage.

Chapter 03 · Values

Four things we will not compromise on.

01

Dignity

Help is offered quietly, respectfully, and without judgement.

02

Confidentiality

Your story stays between us. Always.

03

Community

We believe belonging is half the medicine.

04

Stewardship

Every pound is spent with care — for now and for those who follow.

Walk with us.

Every subscription, every ticket, every quiet donation turns into real help for someone who gave their working life to tea.
