Common questions
Who can attend?

Anyone with a connection to the UK tea trade — past or present. Friends and partners are very welcome too. It's an inclusive, informal evening.

How do I get tickets?

Email us at info@ukttbs.co.uk or click the enquiry button on any event card above. We'll send you everything you need.

What does a ticket include?

Cocktails, canapés and entertainment for the evening. The raffle is purchased separately — either in advance or on the night.

Where does the money go?

100% of proceeds go to the Society's welfare fund. Our trustees are unpaid volunteers, so overheads are negligible. Your money helps people directly.

Can I sponsor a table or donate a raffle prize?

Absolutely — we'd love to hear from you. Get in touch at info@ukttbs.co.uk and we'll discuss options.
