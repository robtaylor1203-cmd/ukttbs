Common questions
How much does it cost?

£120 per year (paid annually) or £10 per month. Both give you one entry per draw — 12 chances to win each year.

Can anyone join?

Yes — the 100 Club is open to anyone, whether or not you've worked in the tea trade. Friends, family and supporters are all welcome.

How are winners chosen?

Winners are selected by a verified random draw each month. The draw is supervised by a trustee and winners are notified directly by email.

Can I cancel?

Absolutely. You can pause or cancel your subscription at any time from your account page, or by contacting us. No awkward phone calls, no questions asked.

Where exactly does my money go?

Every penny goes to the Society's welfare fund. Trustees are unpaid volunteers and our overheads are negligible. Your subscription directly supports members of the trade.

Is it tax deductible?

The 100 Club subscription is a lottery entry, not a donation, so it is not eligible for Gift Aid. However, it is still a tremendously effective way to support the Society.
