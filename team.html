The Team

Volunteer Trustees

Governance

Unpaid. Committed.
Drawn from the trade.

The UKTTBS is steered by a small, volunteer board of trustees. Every trustee has a professional connection to the UK tea industry — past or present — and gives their time freely, without salary or expenses. Between them they bring decades of trade experience and a shared belief that this community looks after its own.
