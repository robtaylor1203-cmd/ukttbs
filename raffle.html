How it's drawn

Fair, recorded, live.

Every ticket sold — whether bought online in advance or from a QR code dotted around the venue — is logged against your account in our central database, with a unique sequential ticket number. There are no paper stubs to lose and no double-counting.

On the night, a Trustee runs the draw from the admin console. The system uses a cryptographically random pick across all entries that haven't already won, records the result in an immutable audit log (with the time, the prize, and the Trustee who drew it), and the winning ticket number is announced from the stage. If you're in the room your name is called; if you're not, we'll be in touch the next morning.

Two draws each year — at the Spring and Autumn cocktail parties — with proceeds going directly to UKTTBS welfare.
