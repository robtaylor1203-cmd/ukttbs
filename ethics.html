Ethics

How we operate

Our commitment

Trust is earned
by the quiet things.

The UKTTBS exists because people in difficult circumstances chose to trust us. That trust is the foundation of everything we do. Every penny is accounted for, every conversation is confidential, and every decision is made with the welfare of members foremost in mind.

01

Absolute Confidentiality

Any member who contacts us for help can be certain that their situation will never be disclosed. Not to other members, not to employers, not to anyone. This is unconditional.

02

Financial Transparency

We publish annual accounts to the Charity Commission and report to our members at the AGM. Every penny raised through events, raffles and the 100 Club is tracked and accounted for — always directed towards welfare work.

03

Volunteer Governance

Every trustee is a volunteer. Nobody draws a salary, nobody claims expenses. The Society's overheads are kept as close to zero as possible so that funds go to the people they are meant for.

04

Dignity Above All

Asking for help takes courage. We make that step as easy and as human as possible. There are no forms, no assessments, no hoops — just a conversation with someone who understands.
